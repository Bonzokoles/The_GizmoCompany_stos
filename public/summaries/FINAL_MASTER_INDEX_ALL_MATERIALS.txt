## INTEGRATED PHILOSOPHICAL FRAMEWORKS

### Framework 1: Master/Slave Morality (Nietzsche)
**Files**: [236], [238], [237]
**Concept**: Slave = reactive, defined against (resentment)
           Master = creative, defined toward (affirmation)
**Application**: 15+ scenarios show real-world Master/Slave choices

### Framework 2: Eternal Recurrence Test (Nietzsche)
**Files**: [236], [238], [266], [267]
**Question**: Would you choose to live this exact moment forever?
**Answers**: YES = authentic, NO = trapped, NEITHER = cosmic perspective
**Examples**: Vegas gambler, terminal patient, artist, junkie

### Framework 3: Dichotomy of Control (Stoic Epictetus)
**Files**: [236], [266], [267], [268]
**Within Control**: Choices, effort, attitude, integrity
**Outside Control**: Outcomes, luck, others' reactions, markets
**Mastery**: Focus on 10% you control, accept 90% you don't
**Real Examples**: Refugee, ex-convict, artist, researcher

### Framework 4: Freedom Without Guarantees (Camus)
**Files**: [266], [267], [268]
**Definition**: Freedom exists precisely WHERE guarantees don't exist
**Practice**: "Acceptance without resignation" - active rebellion
**Examples**: Startup, whistleblower, migrant, poet

### Framework 5: Memento Mori (Stoic + Buddhist)
**Files**: [266], [267], [268]
**Ancient Practice**: Remember you will die
**Function**: Clarity - removes noise, reveals priority
**Levels**: Intellectual → Emotional → Existential → Practical
**Examples**: Terminal patient (ultimate test), exile, scientist

### Framework 6: Will to Power Redefined (Nietzsche)
**Files**: [236], [238], [266], [268]
**Wrong Understanding**: Domination, accumulation
**Correct Understanding**: Creative expression despite lack of guarantee
**Distinction**: Slave Will (seeking approval), Master Will (expressing self)
**Example**: Artist painting without market, researcher without survival guarantee

### Framework 7: Black Humor as Consciousness Tool
**Files**: [236], [237], [238], [266], [267], [268]
**NOT**: Escapism, darkness for darkness sake
**BUT**: Dark jokes that make you see reality clearly
**Function**: Laugh at absurdity THEN CHOOSE ACTION
**Examples**: "20 years = 2 weeks severance", "Free at last, also unemployable"

## KEY INSIGHTS FOR AI

### Insight 1: Freedom ∝ Lack of Guarantee
The more guarantees removed, the more freedom emerges.
Counterintuitive but: Terminal patient (no guarantee of tomorrow) is freer than corporate lawyer (guaranteed tomorrow, imprisoned schedule).

### Insight 2: Authenticity = Accepting Loss
Master morality isn't about winning. It's about expressing integrity despite knowing you'll lose.
The artist paints knowing the painting won't save him. The whistleblower speaks knowing he'll be destroyed.
That's not optimism. That's mastery.

### Insight 3: Dichotomy Liberates
Realizing 90% of life is outside your control is FREEING, not depressing.
Because now you can focus 100% of energy on the 10% that's yours.
That's not resignation. That's strategic power.

### Insight 4: Death Clarifies
Memento Mori isn't morbid. It's practical.
Knowing you're dying removes noise. You suddenly know what matters.
Thompson knew. That's why his writing was so clear.

### Insight 5: Black Humor = Consciousness
When someone makes dark jokes and changes behavior: consciousness-raising.
When someone makes dark jokes and continues harm: escapism.
The joke alone doesn't tell you. Action does.

### Insight 6: Polish Context Matters
Polish literature uniquely combines:
- Totalitarian pressure (dichotomy: resist or collaborate)
- Exile (temporal displacement, witnessing home die)
- Witness imperative (must testify to what you saw)
- This creates specific philosophical flavor different from Western existentialism
